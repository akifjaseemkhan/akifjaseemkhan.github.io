Privacy Policy

GamesByAJK · Last updated 11 November 2026

Summary

Our games do not collect, share or sell any personal data. They work offline and have no accounts, no advertising and no analytics.

Who this applies to

This policy applies to all mobile games published by GamesByAJK on Google Play and other app stores (“we”, “our games”), including games released in the future.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, a game may save things like completed levels, scores, coins, achievements and your settings (for example sound, music and vibration) on your device only. This data does not leave your device through our games and we cannot access it. You can delete it at any time with the game's reset option (if it has one) or by uninstalling the game. If your device's own backup feature (for example Android backup) is turned on, your operating system may include this data in your personal backup; that backup is controlled by you and your device provider, not by us.

Permissions

Our games only use standard permissions needed to run, such as vibration (for optional haptic feedback) and audio settings (for sound effects and music). They do not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Some games can show an optional daily reminder notification, only if you turn it on and allow notifications. It is created and scheduled on your device itself; nothing is sent to us or any server. You can turn it off in the game’s settings or in your phone’s settings at any time.

Third parties

Our games contain no advertising, analytics, tracking or social media services and do not send data to third parties.

Children

Our games do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If any of our games ever adds a feature that handles personal data (such as ads or online play), we will update this page before that version is released, explain what changes for that game, and change the “Last updated” date above.

Contact
